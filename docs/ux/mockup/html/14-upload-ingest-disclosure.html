Ingest and storage
Files uploaded to Discovery are written to MemberJunction Storage and attached to the space library as a tracked item.
Extraction and indexing
Uploaded files are extracted, chunked, and indexed by the Content Pipeline for space-scoped search and assistant answers.
Participant visibility scope
Shared band items are visible to every active member who reaches this space, including external space participants.
Retention and removal boundaries
Removing a participant seat revokes access immediately, and deleting the last item removes indexed chunks and tombstones its content item.
